# Limitations, gaps and conflicting records

## Scientific scope

- Positive results concern bounded mechanics, a frozen parity backbone, supervised canonical-route recovery and narrow maintenance. They do not establish useful route discovery, adaptive depth, deployment efficiency or general language modeling.
- Parity saturated both receiver-anchored arms. Its zero treatment effect and zero distinguishing-operation ablation decreases fail the preregistered research objective despite 100% robust accuracy.
- Keyed-register qualification is incomplete. Online78-update observations cannot replace final development or infer operation relevance.
- The CLRS adapter is a serialized, output-only AGT-plus-pointer implementation of one official task, not a reproduction of the original CLRS GNN or a result on all 30 tasks. Exact-array competence remains absent on the declared panels.
- Frozen bilinear decoding and end-to-end acquisition are different questions. The first probe was nonconverged; its separately certified refit failed competence. This does not prove all bilinear maps or representations incapable.
- The feature-complement intervention was tested as one complete variant under one recipe. More retained variation and an accuracy gain over a weak control did not produce query-conditioned sorting competence. The experiment does not uniquely identify the historical cause of collapse.
- One-array localization/mHC/radial analyses are not independent replications. Effective operator conditioning, first observed divergence, or a large norm difference is not a causal component attribution.
- Finite suffix tests do not guarantee universal committed-state equivalence. Current prediction equality can conceal different future behavior; state cloning alone does not certify semantics.

## Exposure and uncertainty

Several panels were examined historically and reused. They can be held out from a particular readout fit while not being untouched confirmation. Unknown Stage 1 exposure, short-input overlaps and shared backbones remain as recorded in individual manifests. A new name or seed does not erase exposure.

Stochastic tapes are paired repeated outcomes, not new sequences. Queries/tokens/candidate pairs within an array are not independent arrays. Conditional bootstrap intervals do not estimate training-seed reproducibility. A zero-width interval or identical finite outcomes is not proof of population-level equivalence or no opportunity.

Only one successful Stage 1 checkpoint was available for Gate-A's backbone analysis. Controller-seed replication on that shared frozen backbone must not be described as independent backbone replication.

## Development and reproduction limits

Archived `.bak`/notebook checkpoints and older executor paths are not interchangeable with the corrected substrate. Existing tests may assume historical budgets, construct models, execute backward or use hardware-specific paths. Historical full regressions were not uniformly green. No current full-suite verdict is inferred from a subset or a stale report.

Indexed LFS pointers have matching local identities, but large remote payloads were not freshly downloaded for this documentation audit. Checkpoint existence and a source script alone do not guarantee that a new environment can reproduce an experiment. Reproduction must include data exposure, exact initialization, all state/commit semantics, numerical settings and budget boundaries.
